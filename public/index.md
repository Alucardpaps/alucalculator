## Core Solvers & Standards

### 1. Mechanical & Machine Elements
- [Bolt Tightening Torque (VDI 2230)](https://www.alucalculator.com/bolt-torque): Tightening torque, preload clamp load $F_M$, and thread stress calculations conforming to VDI 2230 Blatt 1 and ISO 898-1.
- [Rolling Bearings Rating Life (ISO 281)](https://www.alucalculator.com/bearings): Modified $L_{10}$ and $L_{10h}$ rating life, equivalent dynamic load, and lubrication life for ball and roller bearings.
- [Spur & Helical Gears (ISO 6336)](https://www.alucalculator.com/gears): Tooth bending strength and contact stress (pitting) safety factors per ISO 6336 Method B.
- [Keyway Connections (DIN 6885 / DIN 6892)](https://www.alucalculator.com/keyway-connection): Surface pressure and shear stress analysis for standard rectangular parallel keys.
- [Shaft Stress & Fatigue (DIN 743)](https://www.alucalculator.com/shafts): Multi-stepped shaft safety factors against fatigue under combined torsion and bending.
- [Press Fits & Interference (DIN 7190)](https://www.alucalculator.com/press-fit): Elastic and elastic-plastic contact pressure, transmissible torque, and hub yield safety.
- [Metric Fits & Tolerances (ISO 286)](https://www.alucalculator.com/fits): Fundamental deviations and tolerance grades for hole-basis and shaft-basis systems.

### 2. Structural & FEA
- [Beam Deflection & Bending](https://www.alucalculator.com/beam-deflection): Euler-Bernoulli beam formulas for simply supported, cantilever, and overhanging beams.
- [Column Buckling (Euler / Johnson / AISC)](https://www.alucalculator.com/column-buckling): Critical buckling load $P_{cr}$ and slenderness ratio $\lambda$ across effective length conditions.
- [FEA Linear Static Simulation](https://www.alucalculator.com/fea): WebGL-accelerated 3-template finite element analysis for plates, brackets, and beams.

### 3. Fluids & Thermodynamics
- [Fluid Dynamics & Pipe Friction](https://www.alucalculator.com/fluid-dynamics): Darcy-Weisbach head loss, Colebrook-White friction factor, and pump operating points per Crane TP-410.
- [Pneumatic & Hydraulic Cylinders](https://www.alucalculator.com/cylinders): Push/pull forces, fluid consumption, piston acceleration, and rod buckling safety.
